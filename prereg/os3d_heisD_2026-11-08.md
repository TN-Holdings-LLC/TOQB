## What is compared

The same Trotter evolution as `prereg/os3d_heisB_2026-11-07.md` (4 x 2 x 2, Neel start, U = exp(-i 0.05 (XX + YY +
ZZ)) on every lattice edge, t = 1-4), in three layouts at the same places (the same 18 qubits: two heavy-hex rows of
eight and two bridges):
- "old" (tags h3d): the layout run on 2026-11-07;
- "carry" (h3c): every x-bond rides on the swap of its columns (`gpu/heis3d.py` FoldHeis, carry);
- "line" (h3l): both blocks of a lattice row interleaved on one heavy-hex row, every x- and z-bond on a swap or in
  place (no empty swap), even steps reversed, y-bonds through the bridges, and at step boundaries two y-bonds of the
  same pair merged into one U(2 dt) (`gpu/heis3d.py` physical_line; schedules from `notes/heis_cz_2026-11-08`).
CZ after compiling on the 11-07 fez record (bridges at line positions 1 and 5), t = 1-4: old 176 / 348 / 520 / 692,
carry 152 / 300 / 448 / 596, line 116 / 218 / 320 / 422. On rows whose bridges sit at positions 3 and 7 the line
layout takes 128 / 242 / - / 470 (two empty swaps per step). Each layout has its own bond order, hence its own exact
answers.

## Choice, verdicts and the comparison (`gpu/heis3d_score.py OUT ANS`)

- Place: rule "top" over the mean of the candidate's three t = 1 mirrors, so the choice favours no layout. All three
  layouts are judged there.
- Per judged arm, as on 11-07: READS, AGREES (|m_s - m_s exact| <= 0.05), retention m_s / m_s exact.
- The comparison, per t on the judged place and per new layout: retention (carry) - retention (old) and retention
  (line) - retention (old), as numbers; "resolved" when the size exceeds 2 x sqrt(2) / (sqrt(4000) |m_s exact (old)|)
  (0.048, 0.059, 0.084, 0.145 at t = 1-4), otherwise "within the shot bound". Beside: the same at every other place
  and the mean over places.
- Expected (predictions recorded before the run, not verdicts): line - old >= 0 and carry - old >= 0 at every t on the
  judged place. The simulator's estimate is in the next section.
