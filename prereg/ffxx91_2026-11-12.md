## Question

Within a family, reading stops along depth, at a place set by how fast the exact signal shrinks (Hubbard on 11-11:
read at 48 CZ layers, not at 72, mean square of the answer 0.05 / 0.02 there). The XX chain is exact at any size and
depth and keeps a large signal at theta = pi/4 (mean square 0.26 / 0.20 / 0.18 / 0.17 / 0.17 / 0.18 at 2 / 4 / 8 / 12
/ 16 / 20 steps). How deep does it read on one device-day?

## Prediction (before any data; not rewritten if wrong)

- theta pi/2 READS at every step (2 to 20).
- theta pi/4 READS at least to 12 steps (48 CZ layers). Basis: the same layers read for Hubbard with a signal a third
  as large (0.05 against 0.17).
- 16 and 20 steps (64 and 80 layers): no prediction (open).

## Limits

One device-day; raw values; one chain; theta pi/2 is a Clifford circuit whose answers are +-1, so it shows how much
of a full signal survives, not how the device treats a non-Clifford circuit.
