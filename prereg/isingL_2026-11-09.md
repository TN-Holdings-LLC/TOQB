all spins up at the start, t = 1-4) on 4 x 2 x 2 and 4 x 3 x 2, in two layouts at the same place and on the same
qubits: the fold v3.1 (psf-os default) and the line layout (psf-os 0fb4512, grid3d layout "line", placed with

records). The bonds commute, so the exact answer is the same for both layouts: psf_os.grid3d.exact.

4 x 3 x 2 fold 234 / 468 / 702 / 936, line 126 / 252 / 378 / 504 (t = 1-4).

## Choice, verdicts and the comparison (`gpu/isingL_score.py OUT ANS`)

- Per lattice, place: rule "top" over the mean of the candidate's two t = 1 mirrors (fold, line).
- Main measure (`notes/reporting/layout_comparison_metric.md`): nMSE = mean_i (z_i - e_i)^2 / mean_i e_i^2 per arm; per
  t at the judged place, d = nMSE(line) - nMSE(fold); "resolved" when |d| > 2 sqrt(SE_line^2 + SE_fold^2) (SE by
  propagation from the cards' per-site SE), otherwise "within the shot noise".
- Beside: retention M / M_exact of both (M = mean <Z>), READS (MSE <= mean square / 2) and AGREES (|M - M_exact| <=
  0.05) per arm; every candidate; the sentinel as recorded.
- Expected (a prediction, not a verdict): d <= 0 (the line closer to the exact answer) at every t on both judged
  places.
