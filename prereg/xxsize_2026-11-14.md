## Question

On 2026-11-12 (fez, N = 91) the kept share r = sum z e / sum e^2 was nearly the same at theta = pi/4 and pi/2 at every
depth (0.806 / 0.817 at 8 CZ layers ... 0.219 / 0.194 at 64), and fell by about 1/e every 43 layers; on 2026-11-13
(marrakesh, N = 91, pi/4) it fell by about 1/e every 79 layers. If r is set by the CZ layers a qubit goes through, and
not by the size of the circuit or the size of its signal, then (1) r is the same at pi/4 and pi/2, (2) r is the same at
N = 33 and N = 81, and (3) whether a result reads is told by r alone (r >= 1 - 1/sqrt 2 = 0.293, the line that holds
when the error is a pure shrink).
