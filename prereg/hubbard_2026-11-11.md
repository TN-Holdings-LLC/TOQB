## Question

Does a 40-qubit (L = 20) and a 60-qubit (L = 30) Hubbard chain read raw after 2, 4 and 6 Trotter steps, and does the
U = 0 ruler of the same job, built with the same CZ count and layers, keep the same share of its exact pattern?

## Circuits

`notes/hubbard_2026-11-11/hub_cost.py` (workplace; checked exact against the model, 6e-16): 2L qubits, interleaved
Jordan-Wigner (up, down of site i on qubits 2i, 2i+1), Neel start (site i holds up if i even, down if odd), J 1,
dt 0.25, first-order Trotter: onsite RZZ(U dt / 2) and RZ(-U dt / 2) on both qubits, then even and odd bonds by
fermionic swaps (SWAP, CZ) and XX+YY hopping. Arms:
- `U4`: U = 4;
- `U0` (ruler): U = 0 with each onsite RZZ written as CZ, barrier, CZ (the identity) and the onsite RZ(0) dropped, so
  both arms have the same CZ count and CZ depth (checked by the builder and the pre-run check; refused otherwise).
Steps 2, 4, 6 (J t = 0.5, 1.0, 1.5); L = 20 and 30. 12 circuits, each with its floor and mirror, one ring sentinel,
4000 shots. Build: `gpu/hub_build.py DEV OUT ANS_IN ANS_OUT --L 20 30 --steps 2 4 6`.
