## What is reproduced

Kim et al., Nature 618, 500 (2023), on ibm_kyiv (Eagle, 127 qubits, heavy-hex): |0...0>, t steps of R_X(theta_h) on
every qubit then R_ZZ(-pi/2) on every edge; theta_h = k pi / 32, k = 0..16. Observables (as read from the paper,
`notes/utility_repro_2026-11-10/README.md`):
- `z106_t4`: Z_106 after 4 steps (Fig. 2);
- `w10_t5`: X13 X29 X31 Y9 Y30 Z8 Z12 Z17 Z28 Z32 after 5 steps (Fig. 3);
- `w17_t5`: X37 X41 X52 X56 X57 X58 X62 X79 Y75 Z38 Z40 Z42 Z63 Z72 Z80 Z90 Z91 after 5 steps (Fig. 3);
- `w17b_t5x`: the same with Y and Z exchanged, after 5 steps and one more R_X layer (Fig. 4a).
IBM's values were error-mitigated (ZNE). Here every value is raw: the question is which of these observables read
without mitigation on a Heron device, with the published circuit on the published graph.

## Device and placement (no quality number)

- Right before the fetch, pending jobs of ibm_marrakesh, ibm_fez, ibm_kingston (`gpu/daily_fetch.py`). In the order of
  fewest pending (ties: that order), the first device on which the cones of both `z106_t4` and `w10_t5` avoid every
  failed element (below); none: the first on which `w10_t5`'s does; none: not run.
- Eagle's graph embeds in Heron r2's in two ways (rustworkx VF2, fixed order 0, 1). Per observable, the first
  embedding whose backward light cone (qubits within t edges of the support, and every edge touching them) holds no
  failed qubit or coupler of the record. An observable with no such embedding on the chosen device is not built.
- Gates on failed elements are left out; they lie outside the cone, so the observable's exact value is the published
  circuit's. Otherwise every circuit is the whole embedded circuit.
- Each R_ZZ(-pi/2) as RZ(-pi/2) x RZ(-pi/2) then one CZ (checked: 4e-16). Measured: the observable's qubits only, in
  its basis. Per observable a floor and a mirror at k = 8; one ring sentinel. 4000 shots.
- Build: `gpu/utility_build.py DEV OUT ANS SEALED_DIR`. A device on which the ring sentinel does not land (as
  marrakesh on 11-10) counts as one on which the job cannot be built: the next device in the order.

## Pre-run check (for the night's build, before submission): `gpu/utility_check.py OUT ANS`

Every built observable at k = 0 and 16 (Clifford), the job's own compiled circuit on Aer's stabilizer method over the
whole device: the parity equals the exact value (+-1 exactly; 0 within 4 / sqrt(400)). Z_106 at all 17 k by its
17-qubit cone (the light-cone schedule, compiled the same way): within 1e-9. FAIL means not run.

## Verdicts (`gpu/utility_score.py OUT ANS`)

Per observable over its 17 points: READS if MSE <= mean square of the exact values / 2; per point AGREES if
|z - e| <= 0.05 (counted). Beside, never judged: retention where |e| >= 0.1, floor and mirror parity, sentinel, CZ.
