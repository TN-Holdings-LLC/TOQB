## Device

One device: of the night's daily-record devices (`prereg/daily/standing_rules.md`), the one with the fewest pending
jobs at the fetch (ties: the order of the queue reading). Its fetched record is used for this job too. If no fold
translation qualifies there, or the job is refused, a line "not run" with the reason; nothing else is tried.

## Job (`gpu/os3d_v31_build.py DEVICE_RECORD OUT ANSWERS_OUT`; one job, 4000 shots)

- Probes for the first k = 4 qualifying fold translations in the fixed order (fewer if fewer qualify): the |0> floor,
  the |1> floor and the X echo (|+>, skeleton, barrier, inverse, back) at t = 3, every touched qubit read, bridges
  included.
- 3D arms g3d<i>_t<t>: 4 x 2 x 2 kicked Ising (RZZ(-pi/2) on every lattice edge, then RX(0.8) on every site), v3.1 at
  candidate i, t = 1, 2, 3, each with floor and mirror. Every candidate's arm is in the job.
- 2D arms hex_t<t>: 16 sites of the device's own lattice (a hexagon of 12 and two tails of 2; 16 edges), the same step,
  t = 1, 2, 3, placed by psf-os (user2d, translation), floor and mirror.
- One ring sentinel. On the 11-06 marrakesh record: 61 circuits, 71.1 s by the model.

## Choosing the place (owner 2026-11-06: "white of white", "focus on choosing good places", k = 4, test the bridges)

`psf_os.grid3d.probe_choose(rule="best")`: a qubit is marked when it stands out from the job's own probe readings
(|0> floor or X echo below median - max(5 x 1.4826 MAD, 0.05); |1> floor above median + the same). Among the
candidates with no marked qubit, the judged 3D arm is the candidate whose weakest touched qubit (min of the |0>
floors, the X echoes and minus the |1> floors) reads best; ties: the fixed order. If every candidate has a marked
qubit: the same ranking over all of them, marked "probe passed none". The choice reads only the probes, never an
answer or an arm's error.

## Judged (`gpu/os3d_v31_score.py OUT ANSWERS_OUT best`)

- Per t, for the chosen 3D arm and for the 2D arm: **READS** (MSE <= half the mean square of the exact values),
  **AGREES** (|M - M_exact| <= 0.05), **BEATS** (MSE below the better of every <Z> = 0 and every <Z> = 1).

## Beside (reported, not judged)

Retention M / M_exact, normalised error, decay per CZ layer; CZ and layers of each arm (candidates at an even row
offset use the 10-swap schedule: 148 against 108 CZ per step); floor and mirror; every candidate's probe readings and
marks; every other candidate's scores (all shown; what a pick after the fact would have given stays visible as such);
the price of the dimension (3D / 2D: CZ, layers, retention; the two problems differ, 28 against 16 edges, so their
MSEs are not compared); the sentinel; the calibration beside at the submit and at the run (report only).

Stated in advance: ranking by a measurement favours a candidate that read well by chance. The ranking uses only the
probes (4000 shots, about +-0.01 at a floor near 1) while a faulty element moved its reading by 0.3 or more on 11-06,
so the effect on the judged arm is expected to be small, but it is not zero.

## Procedure

Fetch (the daily record's) -> build here from the fetched record -> the printed answers SHA-256 checked against the list
above -> commit the build -> submit (with the night's daily job, in the same few minutes) -> calibration beside at the

## Limits

One device, one night; one small lattice (16 sites); a device fault of a kind none of the three probes reads (for
instance one that appears only during the arms) is not caught. All answers are classically computable; no advantage
is claimed.
