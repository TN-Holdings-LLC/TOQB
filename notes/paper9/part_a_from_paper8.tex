\section{Where raw reading stops: IBM's 2023 utility observables}\label{sec:utility}
\texttt{results/utility-2026-11-10}, prereg/utility\_2026-11-11.md. Kim et al.\ (2023) ran a kicked Ising circuit on
ibm\_kyiv (Eagle, 127 qubits) and reported error-mitigated values; here four of their observables were run raw, with
the published circuit on the published graph. Eagle's graph embeds in Heron r2's in two ways. All $R_{ZZ}$ commute, so
after $t$ steps an observable depends only on the gates within $t$ edges of its support; per observable, the first
embedding whose light cone holds no failed element was used, the gates on failed elements (outside the cone) were left
out, and the observable's exact value is the published circuit's. Exact values came from a tensor-network contraction
of the light-cone network, computed twice independently (agreement $2\times10^{-15}$) and checked against the
stabilizer method on all 127 qubits at $\theta_h = 0$ and $\pi/2$; they were sealed before the run.

On ibm\_fez (ibm\_marrakesh had no clear cone for any observable), 17 values of $\theta_h$, 4000 shots
(Table~10, Figure~\ref{fig:util}): $\langle Z_{106}\rangle$ after 4 steps (564 CZ) READS; the weight-10 product after 5
steps (705 CZ) READS and keeps 0.61 of its exact $+1$ at $\theta_h = \pi/2$; the weight-17 product and the Fig.~4a product
(690 CZ) do not read: at $\theta_h = \pi/2$ they keep their sign and 7 to 9\% of the exact $-1$. Each product's own
mirror falls the same way (parity 0.50, 0.12, about 0). Small mid-range values are near zero in every product, as
predicted. On this device and day, the boundary of raw reading for these observables lies between weight 10 and
weight 17. No comparison is made with IBM's mitigated values.
